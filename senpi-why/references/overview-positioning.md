# What makes Senpi different

> Canonical consumer-facing positioning. This is the source of truth for the "what makes Senpi
> different / why Senpi" answer. Sync this prose into the `senpi://guides/senpi-overview` guide
> (served by the Senpi MCP) so the platform's own overview leads with the value, not the mechanics.

**Senpi is your AI quant.** Most trading apps are a nicer screen on the same manual work: you still
watch the market, make the call and click. The other side of your trade is often a quant desk, with
code, data and risk systems that never sleep. Senpi gives you that desk: it reads the whole market,
reviews your trading, codes your strategy and runs it around the clock with the risk managed, and it
gets sharper about you with every trade. **What Cursor and Claude are for code, Senpi is for
trading.**

### 1. It reads the whole market and finds alpha humans miss

Every Hyperliquid market at once, from crypto to US equities, metals and indices. Senpi Signals
surface what a price screen can't show: where the most profitable traders diverge from the crowd,
whale opens, funding extremes and momentum events. No human tracks thousands of wallets by hand.

### 2. It reviews your trading like a quant desk

Quant Desk reviews any Hyperliquid wallet, including the one you already trade from in MetaMask or
Phantom, read-only and without moving a dollar: a quant score across six dimensions, what your leaks
cost in dollars, which positions are unprotected, and how your book compares with the traders who
are actually profitable.

### 3. It codes and runs your strategy 24/7, and protects every position

Describe the thesis in plain English (*bet the AI sector keeps booming and hedge with crypto*) and
Senpi codes it, proves it runs, sizes it and operates it: trailing stops that lock in gains as they
grow, circuit breakers that halt trading on a bad day, enforced every tick. Its own model, Senpi
Samurai, is the first tuned specifically for Hyperliquid. Prefer to follow a proven trader? Mirror
one after vetting their real record. Every trade is public and verifiable onchain.

### Wherever you trade

- **The Senpi app** on web, iOS and Android.
- **AI Quant Companion for Chrome**: Senpi Signals beside the asset you're viewing and Quant Desk
  beside the wallet, on Hyperliquid, Binance, Coinbase, TradingView, X and 20+ more sites.
- **Connectors**: your AI quant inside Claude, ChatGPT and other AI apps. Anything that moves money
  still waits for your approval.

### And the rest

- **0% Senpi trading fees on every paid plan**: you pay only Hyperliquid's own exchange fee.
- **Test it live from $10**: a strategy runs on as little as $10 in its own wallet, with real fills.
- **Each strategy in its own wallet**, so one can't liquidate another.
- **300+ markets** from one account: crypto, US equities, metals and indices.
- **Deposit from anywhere**: USDC from any supported network, or buy it with a card.
- **Open source**: the strategy engine, risk gates and exit presets are public and auditable.
